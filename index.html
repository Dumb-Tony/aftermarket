You are a discarded combat robot with six places to bolt something. Everything you kill leaves parts on the floor.

Firing makes heat, and heat stalls you. Firing shoves you backwards. Kills leave scrap that goes cold in twenty seconds. Moving builds charge, and charge is the only thing that pays for a button.

All four are problems. All four are somebody’s engine. Find the part.
